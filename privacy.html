Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 September 2026   •   Last updated: 22 September 2026

Plain-language summary: SalahStreaks is designed around local-first use. Core worship records and settings can remain on your device. Some optional features connect to third-party services, including online Quran/content services, Islamic-calendar services, location/geocoding services, and AI providers you choose. We do not sell your personal information and we do not operate a user-account backend for your worship logs.

1. Who we are

SalahStreaks (“SalahStreaks”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is an Islamic worship companion developed and supported by MO11. Contact: molittle1011@gmail.com or +255 677 532 140.

2. Information stored on your device

Depending on the features you use, the app may store locally:

  • salah, fasting, Quran, adhkar and other worship logs;
  • streaks, points, achievements and statistics;
  • Quran reading progress;
  • app preferences, notification settings, prayer calculation settings and language/theme choices;
  • saved city, coordinates and related prayer-time preferences;
  • notification schedules and reminder settings;
  • AI provider configuration and API key information you choose to save; and
  • backups you explicitly create or export.

We do not receive these core records merely because they are stored in the app.

3. Location

If you grant location permission, SalahStreaks may access your device location to improve prayer-time and Islamic-calendar calculations. You may instead enter or select a city where the app supports that option.

When an online service is used for a location-dependent feature, coordinates or location-derived information may be transmitted to that service. This is necessary to obtain the requested calculation or calendar information. The third party's privacy policy applies to its processing.

4. Online Quran and Islamic content

Some versions of SalahStreaks may retrieve Quran text, translations, tafsir or related content from online services so the Daily Verse feature can draw from a larger corpus and reduce repetitive selections. When this happens, the device makes a network request to the relevant content provider. We do not use those requests to build a SalahStreaks advertising profile.

5. Islamic calendar and upcoming events

Upcoming Islamic events may be retrieved or calculated using date, timezone and location-related information so the app can display dates appropriate to the user's selected location. Islamic dates can differ by calculation method and by local moon-sighting decisions. Users should verify important observances with their trusted local authority.

6. Notifications

SalahStreaks uses local device notifications for reminders. Scheduled notification information is maintained by the operating system on the user's device. The app does not require a remote push-notification server for its local prayer, Quran or adhkar reminders.

7. Optional AI assistant

The AI assistant is optional. If you configure an AI provider using your own API key, your prompts and any information you send in an AI conversation are transmitted from your device to the provider or endpoint you selected. The provider may process and retain that information under its own terms and privacy policy.

  • SalahStreaks does not provide you with a shared app-wide AI key.
  • You are responsible for protecting your API key and for any provider charges.
  • Do not send highly sensitive personal information to an AI provider unless you understand and accept that provider's practices.
  • AI output can be incorrect and is not a substitute for qualified religious, medical, legal or other professional advice.

8. Third-party services

Depending on your use and the version of the app, third-party services may support Quran/content retrieval, Islamic-calendar data, geocoding/location services, AI processing, app-store distribution, or operating-system functionality. Those providers process information according to their own terms and policies. We encourage you to review the policies of any service you choose to use.

9. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not require a SalahStreaks account for core app use.
  • We do not operate a central database containing your worship logs and streaks.
  • We do not intentionally use worship activity for advertising profiling.

These statements describe the current architecture and may change if a future feature introduces accounts, analytics, cloud backup, advertising, or another data-processing service. We will update this policy when material changes occur.

10. Data security

Local data security depends in part on your device's security controls. Use a device passcode/biometric lock and keep your operating system updated. If you export a backup, you are responsible for protecting the exported file. Losing the device, clearing app storage or uninstalling the app can remove local data that has not been backed up.

11. Your choices

  • Grant or revoke location permission through your device settings.
  • Enable or disable reminders in the app or through system notification settings.
  • Remove or change your AI configuration and API key.
  • Clear app data or uninstall the app to remove local app data, subject to your device and backup configuration.
  • Contact us if you have a privacy question or request.

12. Children's privacy

SalahStreaks is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the core app does not require an account, we do not intentionally build child profiles. Parents or guardians who have concerns may contact us.

13. International processing

Third-party services used by optional features may process information in countries other than your own. By using a feature that connects to a third-party service, you acknowledge that the service may process information according to its own policies and applicable law.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, services, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date will identify the current version. Material changes may also be communicated through the app or app-store listing where appropriate.
