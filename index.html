Privacy-first by design.

SalahStreaks is built so core worship records can remain on your device. No account is required for the core experience.

  • Local worship logs, streaks and preferences
  • Local notification scheduling for reminders
  • Optional location access for prayer times and events
  • Optional AI using a key you configure
  • No sale of personal information
Important: Some optional features connect to third-party services to retrieve Quran content, Islamic-calendar information, geocoding, or AI responses. Their processing is governed by their own policies. Read the full Privacy Policy before using those features.

Islamic content note

SalahStreaks is a tool for personal worship and organization. Prayer-time calculations and Islamic-calendar dates can vary by calculation method, local authority and moon-sighting practice. For matters requiring religious judgment, consult a qualified scholar or trusted local authority.
