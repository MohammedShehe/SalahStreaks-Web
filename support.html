Before contacting support

  • Check that your app is updated to the latest available version.
  • For notification issues, verify system notification permission and any battery/alarms settings that may restrict scheduled notifications.
  • For prayer times, verify your location, timezone, calculation method and madhab settings.
  • For AI issues, verify your provider, API key, endpoint and model configuration. Never send your API key in a public support post.
  • For Islamic dates, remember that local moon-sighting decisions can differ from calculated calendar dates.

Technical and development questions

For development, app ownership, technical assistance, bug reports or questions that the in-app AI cannot answer, contact MO11 directly.
