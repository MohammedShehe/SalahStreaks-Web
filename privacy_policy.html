Privacy Policy

SalahStreaks (“the App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”)
Effective date: 31 August 2026
Last updated: 31 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SalahStreaks collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our mobile application. SalahStreaks is an Islamic worship tracking app that helps you log salah, adhkar, Quran progress, and other ibadat, view streaks and statistics, receive local prayer reminders, and optionally chat with an AI assistant using your own API key.

Summary (plain language):

  • Almost all of your data stays on your device.
  • We do not operate a user account system or a central server that stores your worship logs.
  • Location is used only if you allow it (or enter a city) to calculate prayer times on-device.
  • The optional AI Bot sends your chat messages directly to the AI provider you choose, using the API key you provide. We never see or store those keys or chats.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Data stored only on your device

The App stores the following information locally on your device (using on-device databases and preferences):

  • Worship / ibadat logs (e.g., salah, sawm, qiyam, Quran, sadaqah entries and timestamps)
  • Streaks, points, achievements, and related progress statistics
  • Quran reading progress
  • App settings (theme, notification preferences, calculation method, madhab, language, etc.)
  • Optional city name and coordinates you choose for prayer-time calculation
  • Optional AI Bot settings (provider choice, API key, base URL, model) — stored only on your device
  • Backup files you explicitly export

This data never leaves your device unless you export a backup yourself or use the optional AI Bot feature (see below).

1.2 Location information (optional)

To calculate accurate prayer times, the App may request access to your device’s location (via the operating system’s location services) or use a city name you enter manually.

  • Coordinates and city name are used solely to compute prayer times with an on-device calculation library.
  • They are stored only on your device as part of your settings.
  • If you deny location permission, you can still use the App by entering a city name or relying on a previously saved / default location.
  • Reverse-geocoding or address lookup (when you type a city) may involve the device’s platform geocoding services.

1.3 Notifications

The App uses local (on-device) notifications for prayer and adhkar reminders. Notification content and scheduling data remain on your device. We do not send push notifications from a remote server.

1.4 Optional AI Bot (user-supplied API key)

If you enable the AI Bot feature and enter your own API key, chat messages you send are transmitted directly from your device to the AI provider you select (for example Groq, Google Gemini, xAI, or another OpenAI-compatible endpoint you configure).

  • The App does not ship any API keys and does not route traffic through our servers.
  • Your API key and conversation history for the AI Bot are stored only on your device (unless the third-party provider retains data under its own policy).
  • We do not receive, store, or have access to the content of your AI chats or your API keys.

Use of any third-party AI service is subject to that provider’s own terms and privacy policy. You are responsible for complying with those terms and for any costs associated with your API key.

1.5 Information we do not collect

  • We do not require you to create an account or provide an email address, name, or phone number to use the core features of the App.
  • We do not operate a backend that receives your ibadat logs, streaks, or settings.
  • We do not intentionally collect advertising identifiers for advertising purposes, nor do we sell personal data.
  • In the current version of the App we do not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs that transmit personal data to us. If this changes in a future version, we will update this Policy and the Play Console Data safety section.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by the App is used only to:

  • Provide and improve core features (logging worship, streaks, graphs, history, achievements)
  • Calculate and display prayer times and schedule local reminders
  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Enable the optional AI assistant when you choose to use it with your own key
  • Allow you to export or clear your data from within the App

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Data may leave your device only in these limited cases:

  • AI Bot (optional): Your chat messages go directly to the AI provider you selected, under that provider’s privacy policy.
  • Platform / OS services: Location and geocoding requests are handled by the device operating system and its providers (subject to their policies and your permission settings).
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, or property, though given our architecture we generally do not hold user content on our systems.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Primary storage is local to your device (on-device database and preferences).
  • You can export a backup of your data from Settings and store it wherever you choose.
  • You can clear / reset app data from within Settings or by clearing the app’s data via your device settings.
  • We recommend keeping your device locked and up to date. Because most data resides only on your device, loss or theft of the device (or clearing app data) can result in permanent loss of that data unless you have exported a backup.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App in a way that concerns you, please contact us so we can help address it.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Location: You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings. You can also clear or change the saved city/coordinates in the App’s Settings.
  • Notifications: You can disable prayer and adhkar reminders inside the App or at the system level.
  • AI Bot: You can leave the feature disabled, remove your API key, or change providers at any time.
  • Data deletion: Use the in-app options to clear data, or uninstall the App / clear app storage on your device. Because we do not maintain a central account database of your worship logs, deletion is performed on-device.
  • Export: You may export a backup of your data from Settings for your own records.

Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws (for example access, correction, deletion, or restriction). Contact us using the details below to exercise any such rights.

7. Third-Party Services

The App may interact with the following categories of third parties solely when the related feature is used:

  • AI providers you configure (Groq, Google Gemini, xAI, or a custom OpenAI-compatible endpoint) — only when the AI Bot is enabled and you supply a key.
  • Device platform services for location, geocoding, notifications, and fonts (e.g., Google Fonts if loaded by the UI framework).

Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. We encourage you to review them.

8. International Users

The App is designed so that core worship data remains on your device. When you use optional features that contact third-party services (AI providers or platform geocoding), those services may process data in other countries. By using those features you acknowledge that such transfers may occur under the terms of the relevant provider.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy. For material changes we will make reasonable efforts to notify users (for example via an in-app notice or an updated listing on Google Play).
